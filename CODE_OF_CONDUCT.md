# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in rag-favorite a respectful,
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity,
experience, or level of contribution.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Keep reviews focused on the work, not the person.
- Protect private data and responsibly disclose security issues.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and sustained
disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers
may edit or remove contributions, lock discussions, issue warnings, or restrict
participation when needed. Reports will be reviewed promptly, fairly, and as
confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents
the project elsewhere.
